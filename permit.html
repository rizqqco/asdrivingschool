Getting a Permit

Documents Required

To obtain your NJ learner's permit, bring the following documents to the DMV:

  • Original birth certificate or US passport (expired not more than 3 years)
  • High School waiver card (blue squared card)
  • Social Security card, copy, or ITIN Number
  • Proof of address

Need help navigating the permit process? We're here to guide you every step of the way. Contact us with any questions.
